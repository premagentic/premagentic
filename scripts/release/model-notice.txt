The embedding model in this folder

model.onnx and vocab.txt are the ONNX export and the vocabulary of
sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2, taken unchanged from
https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
at revision @REVISION@.

The model is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0, as its model
card states. The full text of that license is LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt, beside
this file. The model's repository publishes no NOTICE file.

prem-model.json is written by PremAgentic's release build. It names the
model, its pooling and its dimensions, and records the revision above.

Both files were checked against the SHA-256 values that
scripts/download-model.sh pins, before this archive was made.
